# Borrowed from other OptChat builds, 1 November 2026

A second look at the builds after Taelin's recipe, since the [first
one](2026-10-14-borrowed.md): UniiChat and its forks, pi-optchat,
omp-optchat, opencode-optchat, orrery and others. Most of what they added
since, opt-chat had already. These four it didn't.

## Summaries carry no lists from their context

In arteeh's UniiChat fork, lines ended with "Owed:" lists of open items
that no input held: the compactor copied them from the lines before it, and
each new line copied them again. They went stale, and the agent apologized
for "mistakes" the user had approved
([arteeh/uniichat#13](https://github.com/arteeh/uniichat/issues/13)). The
prompt already said to use `<chat>` "never to add what `<input>` lacks".
It now names the failure: a compaction never carries into its line a list,
a status or a verdict that only `<chat>` holds, even when the lines before
it carry one, and two merged lines that repeat one list hold it once.

**Measured** live, on a made-up chat of 11 messages: a plan of three items,
each settled by the user later, and lines that kept copying the plan as
"Owed: ..." after that, as in arteeh's chat. The compaction was of the
last message, a `grep` result that names none of the items. A line
counted as stale when it named any of them. 10 runs a prompt, bare, on
the same input:

| model, effort | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| Haiku, `high` | 8 of 10 | 1 of 10 |
| Opus, `medium` | 5 of 10 | 0 of 10 |

The one stale line after the change said "Owed items unchanged in chat"
and listed none. Sonnet, the default, was not run. The prompt is part of
every call's cached prefix, so the first calls after the change write it
again.

## Wake-ups that repeat

`wake(at, message, every)` sets a wake-up that comes again every `every`,
until canceled (arteeh's scheduled prompts,
[UniiChat #34](https://github.com/VictorTaelin/UniiChat/pull/34)).

- **every:** a duration (`90m`, `24h`) or whole days (`7d`), at least
  `10m`, since each one starts a turn.
- **Whole days go by the calendar:** a daily one at 09:00 stays at 09:00
  when the clocks change. Any other `every` is added as it is.
- **Without at,** the first comes `every` from now.
- **Each one says what it is:** `[opt-chat] wake-up you set at <time>,
  every 24h (id 3): <message>`, so Opt can cancel it.
- **Missed while opt-chat was down:** it comes once as it starts, then at
  its next time after now, never once for each time missed.

## A run of messages in one zoom

`zoom(id, 1, count)` gives count messages from id on, whole, with their
images, where `zoom(id, 1)` gives one. That is a stretch of the chat word
for word in one call instead of one call a message (samoylenkodmitry's
[optchat](https://github.com/samoylenkodmitry/optchat), `thread(id, n)`).

- **Size:** up to 64 KB in all, an image counting as 6 KB (about its
  tokens as text), though the first message always comes whole. A message past that is not cut: the call says which messages it
  left out and the zoom that goes on.
- **The chat's own lookups:** a `zoom`, `search` or `date` result shows as
  left out, as `search` leaves them out. The call itself shows.
- **count with n above 1** is refused.

## The compactions' view survives a restart

Every compaction opens with the compactions' view, the same lines for all
that run at once, so that they read it from the cache. On a restart it was
built again from the chat's view: its lines up to the first message not
summarized, merged down to 16 KB. Once it had had batches of its own since
the chat's view last merged, that merged other pairs than it had live, so
the compactions after each restart or `/reload` wrote their prefix anew.
`TestCviewSurvivesRestart` showed it: after 160 messages, the rebuilt view
differed from the live one.

`view.json` now keeps the compactions' view too, its keys and whether its
batch is cut short, and is written when that view merges. At start it comes
back as it was, then takes the lines built since, unless the chat's view
merged since the save: then it starts from the chat's view, as it would
have. One whose lines don't tile the chat or aren't all built, or an older
`view.json` without it, is built as before. Source: [pi-optchat
#206](https://github.com/jonaslsaa/pi-optchat/pull/206), after UniiChat's
`seen`.
